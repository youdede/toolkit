# Assess Learning Gaps

## Contract

Assessment is diagnostic, not performative. Calibrate to the learner’s goals, ask one question at a time, and distinguish missing recall from missing understanding or application.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the learning mission, desired depth, domain, prior records, and how results will be used. Agree on question count and whether answers should be stored.
2. Start with a representative task, then adapt difficulty and topic from each answer. Sample concepts, systems, failure modes, transfer, and judgment rather than trivia or syntax alone.
3. After each answer, give a transparent rating rubric, correct only material gaps, and ask a retrieval or application follow-up when needed. Preserve the learner’s wording if records are authorized.
4. Summarize strengths, recurring gaps, confidence limits, and a prioritized plan with observable learning outcomes. Do not overfit a broad judgment to a small sample.

## Validation

- Each claimed gap is supported by a specific response or task performance.
- The learning plan connects gaps to the user’s stated mission.

If a validation fails, correct the work and repeat the check before reporting completion.

## Stop conditions

Stop or change method when the user declines persistent records, the domain requires a qualified examiner, or fatigue invalidates the assessment.

## Refusals

- It does not infer intelligence, employability, or broad competence from a short quiz.
- It does not flatter, shame, or use hidden grading criteria.
- It does not store verbatim answers or personal assessments without consent.
