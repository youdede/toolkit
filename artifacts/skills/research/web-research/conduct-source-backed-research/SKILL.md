# Conduct Source Backed Research

## Contract

Use any approved research surface; no vendor or paid API is required. Every material claim must be traceable to a source that directly supports it.

## Workflow

1. Frame one decision question, context, timeframe, geography, constraints, and three to six coverage questions. Define what evidence would change the decision.
2. Search broadly, then prefer official documentation, standards, filings, datasets, papers, and first-party changelogs. Use forums and social posts only as attributed experience or leads.
3. Open and inspect sources rather than relying on snippets. Record claim, URL, publication and event dates, source type, and confidence; corroborate consequential claims where independent sources exist.
4. Synthesize an answer-first report. Separate confirmed facts, inference, disagreement, and unknowns. Run a gap pass for uncovered questions, stale evidence, contradictions, and single-source claims before finishing.

## Validation

- Each material factual claim has a nearby direct citation.
- Every coverage question is answered or explicitly marked unresolved with the search attempted.

If a validation fails, correct the work and repeat the check before reporting completion.

## Stop conditions

Stop when required sources are inaccessible, the evidence is too weak for the requested confidence, or research would cross a paywall or credential boundary without approval.

## Refusals

- It does not fabricate citations, quote search snippets as evidence, or force consensus.
- It does not rely on a single vendor, API, or model when alternatives are available.
- It does not present inference, forecasts, or forum anecdotes as confirmed fact.
