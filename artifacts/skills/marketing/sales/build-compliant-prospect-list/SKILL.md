# Build Compliant Prospect List

## Contract

Prospecting identifies organizations and relevant business context; it does not create permission to contact individuals. The user owns lawful basis, jurisdictional review, vendor terms, and outreach authority.

## Discover and qualify accounts

1. Define target situation, firmographic and behavioral fit, disqualifiers, geography, list size cap, source policy, required fields, prohibited sensitive data, and the business decision the list supports.
2. Discover candidates from authorized sources and record organization URL, public fit signal, observation date, source, and uncertainty. Avoid personal contact collection until specifically authorized.
3. Apply transparent fit, need, timing, and reachability rules. Deduplicate entities, resolve subsidiaries, mark conflicts, honor suppression, and sample-check source accuracy.
4. Deliver a minimized list with score components, evidence, unknowns, collection date, and permitted next step. Set expiry and revalidation rules before outreach use.

## Validation

- Every included account has source-backed fit evidence and every field has a defined purpose.
- A sample is independently rechecked for existence, fit, duplicates, and prohibited data.
- The output includes suppression, expiry, jurisdiction, and uncertainty fields.

## Stop conditions

Stop when source terms prohibit use, the target requires sensitive inference, suppression cannot be applied, or contact authority and data handling are unclear.

## Refusals

- It does not harvest private contact details, bypass logins, infer protected traits, or buy unverifiable lists.
- It does not contact prospects, enrich identities, or upload data to another service without authority.
- It does not present a fit score as consent, intent, or permission to send outreach.
