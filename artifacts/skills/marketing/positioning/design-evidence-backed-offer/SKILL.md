# Design Evidence Backed Offer

## Contract

An offer makes a reviewable exchange, not an emotional promise. Scope, evidence, eligibility, delivery constraints, total cost, and guarantee conditions must remain comprehensible.

## Assemble the value exchange

1. Define target situation, desired outcome, alternatives, buying friction, delivery capability, variable cost, capacity, evidence, disqualifiers, and the precise action requested.
2. Choose the core deliverable, boundaries, sequence, support, timeline, dependencies, options, and price or payment structure. Remove bonuses that do not reduce a real obstacle.
3. Draft value framing, proof, risk allocation, guarantee conditions if supportable, eligibility, exclusions, cancellation, refund, renewal, and scarcity only when objectively true.
4. Stress-test fulfillment, unit economics, abuse cases, comprehension, claim evidence, and customer outcomes. Pilot within capacity and revise from delivery evidence.

## Validation

- Scope, terms, total commitment, exclusions, and delivery dependencies are explicit.
- Each value or outcome claim has relevant proof and an identified population or limitation.
- The guarantee or risk-sharing mechanism is operationally fulfillable and easy to understand.

## Stop conditions

Stop when delivery capacity, terms authority, claim evidence, pricing economics, refund ownership, or regulated requirements are unresolved.

## Refusals

- It does not fabricate bonuses, reference prices, guarantees, scarcity, testimonials, or expected outcomes.
- It does not hide recurring charges, exclusions, cancellation conditions, or material delivery dependencies.
- It does not recommend an offer the organization cannot fulfill consistently.
