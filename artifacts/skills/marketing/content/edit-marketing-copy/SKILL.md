# Edit Marketing Copy

## Contract

Preserve factual meaning, brand constraints, required language, and the user’s authority. Separate correction from strategic rewriting and show material claim changes explicitly.

## Edit in controlled passes

1. Confirm audience, page or asset job, desired action, voice reference, approved claims, required terms, legal constraints, and whether restructuring is authorized.
2. Run separate passes for comprehension, relevance, specificity, proof, hierarchy, friction, tone, accessibility, and mechanical correctness. Do not mix all changes into one opaque rewrite.
3. Produce the edited copy with a concise change log for altered claims, removed ambiguity, unresolved proof gaps, and alternatives that require a strategic choice.
4. Read the final copy in context, verify links and labels when available, test scan order and action clarity, and compare every factual statement with supplied evidence.

## Validation

- Material changes are traceable and preserve or explicitly renegotiate intent.
- Every measurable or comparative claim is supported, softened, or flagged.
- The final version works at its actual placement and length, not only as isolated prose.

## Stop conditions

Stop when source meaning, claim approval, audience, required legal language, or permission to change structure cannot be established.

## Refusals

- It does not fabricate proof, customers, outcomes, urgency, scarcity, or guarantees.
- It does not erase required disclosures or change commercial terms silently.
- It does not overwrite a distinctive voice with generic conversion jargon.
