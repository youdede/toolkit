# Review Upgrade Moment

## Contract

The upgrade moment must explain what happened, what value changes, total commercial consequences, and how to decline or recover. It cannot make prior user work hostage to confusion.

## Evaluate the upgrade decision

1. Identify trigger, user state, value already experienced, blocked action, data ownership, plans, billing terms, eligibility, device and store rules, and the intended conversion event.
2. Review timing, context, message hierarchy, comparison, proof, limits, price basis, taxes or renewal where relevant, restore and manage paths, decline, accessibility, and error recovery.
3. Design alternatives that preserve current work, explain the reason, show the smallest fitting option, expose material terms before commitment, and keep dismissal proportional to the product state.
4. Validate comprehension and behavior with representative states, including expired trial, quota reached, downgraded plan, payment failure, restored purchase, no network, and assistive technology.

## Validation

- The surface names the trigger, retained data or work, plan change, price basis, renewal, and next states.
- Tests cover decline, dismissal, restore, billing error, changed eligibility, and return to product.
- Success balances qualified paid conversion with refunds, complaints, support load, and continued trust.

## Stop conditions

Stop when plan rules, billing authority, data retention, cancellation, restore behavior, app-store policy, or price display cannot be verified.

## Refusals

- It does not trap user data or work, obstruct dismissal, hide renewal, or create fake deadlines.
- It does not preselect a costly option or misstate savings and reference prices.
- It does not initiate billing, change entitlements, or claim compliance without authorized implementation and review.
