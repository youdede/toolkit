# Evaluate Free Tool Growth

## Contract

The utility must solve a complete small problem before asking for value in return. Evaluate user need, distribution, maintenance, data handling, and product relevance together.

## Test the utility thesis

1. Define target user, recurring job, current workaround, input sensitivity, useful output, desired next action, distribution path, and strategic connection to the paid product.
2. Research demand and competing utilities, then score usefulness, differentiation, build cost, abuse risk, maintenance, support, accessibility, and defensibility.
3. Design the smallest complete interaction with transparent inputs, immediate output, optional save or share, privacy defaults, error states, and a non-blocking product bridge.
4. Specify a validation prototype and measures for qualified use, repeated value, sharing, downstream fit, cost per useful outcome, and maintenance burden.

## Validation

- The proposal proves a real job and a reachable audience rather than assuming SEO demand.
- The MVP includes privacy, accessibility, abuse, error, and maintenance requirements.
- Success criteria include useful completion and downstream fit, not only visits or captured emails.

## Stop conditions

Stop when the output cannot be trustworthy, inputs require unsafe sensitive data, distribution is speculative, or ongoing maintenance has no owner.

## Refusals

- It does not gate the core result behind surprise lead capture.
- It does not present estimates as professional, legal, medical, or financial determinations.
- It does not recommend a thin clone or mass-generated utility with no distinct user value.
