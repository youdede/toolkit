# Design Qualified Lead Magnet

## Contract

The resource must deliver the promised value without withholding the useful core. Qualification comes from problem relevance and voluntary behavior, not excessive form fields.

## Connect value to qualified intent

1. Define audience, urgent job, existing workaround, desired outcome, product connection, proof available, distribution, privacy requirements, and the signal that indicates genuine fit.
2. Select a format that lets the user complete a meaningful task. Outline the artifact, concrete deliverable, evidence, examples, accessibility, update cadence, and production owner.
3. Design an honest promise, minimal optional capture, consent language, immediate delivery, share path, and follow-up that continues the same job rather than switching to generic promotion.
4. Prototype with representative users, verify completion and usefulness, then measure qualified use, activation, follow-up relevance, complaints, and maintenance cost.

## Validation

- The resource solves a bounded job before any sales conversation.
- Capture fields, consent, delivery, accessibility, and suppression behavior are explicit.
- Measurement distinguishes downloads from completed use and qualified downstream behavior.

## Stop conditions

Stop when the resource lacks distinct value, required evidence, maintenance ownership, safe data handling, or a lawful relevant follow-up path.

## Refusals

- It does not disguise an advertisement as neutral research or fabricate benchmark data.
- It does not require unnecessary personal data or preselect promotional consent.
- It does not withhold the promised core behind a sales call or surprise paywall.
