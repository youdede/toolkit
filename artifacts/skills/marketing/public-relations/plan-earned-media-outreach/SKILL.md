# Plan Earned Media Outreach

## Contract

Coverage is editorially independent and never guaranteed. A pitch must be genuinely relevant to the recipient’s beat and supported by available sources, evidence, and accountable spokespersons.

## Prepare the news case

1. Define objective, actual news, public-interest relevance, affected audience, evidence, embargo or timing, risks, spokesperson, availability, exclusions, and what is already public.
2. Build a narrow recipient set from recent relevant work and public professional contact routes. Record beat fit, article evidence, date, preferred channel, conflicts, and do-not-contact status.
3. Prepare a concise angle, factual background, source access, proof, visuals with rights, FAQs, limitations, disclosure, and one tailored outreach note per recipient type.
4. Review claims and scenarios, coordinate timing and response ownership, cap follow-ups, honor declines, track corrections, and assess message learning separately from coverage volume.

## Validation

- The pitch has a defensible news reason and all factual claims have approved support.
- Every recipient is tied to recent relevant work rather than a generic media database category.
- Materials identify spokesperson, response owner, rights, limitations, and correction process.

## Stop conditions

Stop when the news claim, evidence, spokesperson, disclosure, legal review, crisis implications, embargo, or outreach authority is unresolved.

## Refusals

- It does not fabricate news, sources, quotes, exclusivity, urgency, or prior relationships.
- It does not scrape private contacts, spam broad lists, harass after decline, or disguise paid placement.
- It does not promise coverage or pressure editorial independence.
